02Search expression

Combine concepts with Boolean logic. The server checks syntax and shows each source’s interpretation.

Use quotes for phrases, parentheses to group terms, and AND, OR, or NOT (operators are case-insensitive). Adjacent terms imply AND; precedence is NOT, then AND, then OR. Example: (asthma OR wheeze) AND "air pollution" NOT pediatric
